Services

Three focused offerings, each adaptable to where your project is right now.

📄

Manuscript development

For researchers with results in hand who need a strong, submission-ready paper.

  • Journal & conference selection strategy
  • Structuring abstract, intro, methods, results, discussion
  • Strengthening the argument and contribution narrative
  • Figure/table clarity and technical language editing
  • Response-to-reviewer drafting support
🎓

Thesis development

End-to-end support for MS and PhD theses, chapter by chapter.

  • Thesis outline and chapter planning
  • Literature review structuring and synthesis
  • Methodology and results chapter development
  • Formatting to university/department guidelines
  • Defense preparation and mock Q&A
🧭

Research consultancy

Strategic guidance for research design, analysis, and project direction.

  • Research question framing and scoping
  • Methodology and experimental design review
  • Data analysis and statistical approach guidance
  • Research proposal and grant narrative support
  • Project-level review for labs and teams
FAQ

Common questions

How does engagement typically start?

Share your project via the chat assistant or contact form. We review it and follow up with a proposed scope and timeline — no cost or commitment for the initial conversation.

Do you work with non-native English writers?

Yes — a large share of our work is helping researchers communicate strong technical results clearly and confidently in English.

Can you help with a single chapter or section, not the whole thesis/paper?

Absolutely. Engagements range from a single chapter review to full manuscript or thesis development.

What engineering domains do you cover?

Civil, Mechanical, Electrical & Electronics, Computer Science & AI, Environmental, Biomedical, Chemical, and related interdisciplinary areas. See the Domains page.
